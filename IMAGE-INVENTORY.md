## What the reference photos actually show

Re-checked on 2026-09-29 after generation was stopped. The folder on disk is `orignal img`.

Supported by the photographs:

- Closed device: tall matte-black body, chevron-quilted lid, gold monogram, gold rim, small front latch, droplet button, gold script near the base.
- Open lid: the lid stays on its hinge. The top deck is glossy black. Exactly three small colored dots are visible in a triangle. No reference shows a cartridge installed in that deck.
- Cartridges appear only standing beside the device, usually in a group of three, or as a larger shade row. They are not shown being held over the device, pushed in, or connected to it.
- The back of the body has a vertical recess and a USB-C port. No reference shows a brush, liquid, or cartridge coming out of that recess.
- Packaging photos show the quilted lid in the upper tray and the body in the lower tray.
- The app reference shows the device beside a phone with three overlapping color circles.

Not shown, so it must not be staged:

- Insertion, injection, or a hand placing a cartridge into a hole.
- Cartridges seated inside the device.
- Syringe, dropper, pump, pipe, or refill-bottle geometry.
- Liquid moving through the machine, drops, or a mixing well.
- A separate brush laying down a smear as the “dispensing” step.
